## Using artificial intelligence (AI) in your research and manuscript preparations

## Taylor & Francis Editorial Policies

The rapid advancement of artificial intelligence has created new opportunities and considerations for researchers and authors. As AI tools become increasingly sophisticated and widely available, understanding when and how they can be appropriately used in research and manuscript preparation is essential.

This page provides guidance on permissible scenarios for AI use in your research workflow and writing process. We recognize that AI technology and best practices continue to evolve, so we encourage you to review this guidance regularly to stay informed of any updates.

Please bookmark our AI policy to keep up to date with the latest guidance.

## How to declare generative AI use in your manuscript

Include a statement disclosing whether and how you used generative AI in your research or manuscript preparation. Your statement should cover:

The name and version number of the AI tool used.

Confirmation the authors have checked terms of use for the specific AI tool used, and therefore confirm suitability for publication.

If you didn't use generative AI, state that clearly. For example: ' The authors report generative AI was not used in their research or preparation of this manuscript '.
