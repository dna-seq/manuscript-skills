AI Discovery Assistant

## Reviewer Instructions

Use of artificial intelligence (AI) and large language models (LLMs)

## Before you prepare your review

Carefully read the ancillary material, including the Instructions to Reviewers (this document) in full and journal policies on ethics and COIs, use of artificial intelligence (AI), manuscript transfer, co-reviews, etc., and let the editorial office know if you have any questions.

We rely on reviewers for conducting reviews in accordance with and in order to uphold the standards of the journal. Use of AI tools, including LLMs, to prepare your review is not allowed as these types of tools and resources should not act as a substitute for your expert opinion and should not supersede your own judgment. Also, reviewers should not use AI tools to screen manuscripts, even if they suspect text/images were generated by AI.

## Content issues

If you believe there is plagiarism, simultaneous (concurrent) submission, previous publication, image manipulation, citations to content published in predatory journals, suspect artificial intelligence may have been used without disclosure, or you have identified scientific fraud issues in the manuscript you are reviewing, please contact the Editorial Office immediately. Please provide examples of the issues you are concerned about to help the Editors investigate the matter.
