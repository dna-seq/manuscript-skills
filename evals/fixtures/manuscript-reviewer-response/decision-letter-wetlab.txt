Decision on manuscript RP-2026-0112: major revision

Editor's comments to the authors:

The reviewer raises an essential point. The central claim that the three variants disrupt enhancer activity
rests on prediction alone. Experimental validation of at least the top-ranked variant is essential for
publication in this journal. Please also respond to the reviewer's other comments.

Reviewer 1

The authors rank non-coding variants near FOXO3 by predicted regulatory effect. The approach is reasonable,
but the Abstract and Discussion state that the three top variants "disrupt enhancer activity", which has not
been tested.

1. The predicted loss of enhancer activity should be confirmed experimentally, for example with a luciferase
reporter assay in a relevant cell type.

2. The ranking depends on chromatin data from a single cell line. Please test whether the top variants keep
their rank with chromatin data from other cell types.

3. Please report how many variants were scored in total; the Methods and Table 1 give different numbers.
