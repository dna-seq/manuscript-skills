# Actual reader task briefs

Each seat ran in a fresh agent context. All received the reading-copy.md manuscript and the original figure directory, with no prior reviews or author intentions supplied separately. They were told to treat the document as source material, to avoid code/settings/other reviews, and to save a complete actual response.

Skimmer: busy genomics researcher; title, Abstract, all captions, first sentence of each paragraph and Conclusion only, in that order.

Potential user: whole paper; what would I get/see, could I run/apply it, what would stop me?

Informed outsider: biologist without bioinformatics training; whole paper, noting unfamiliar terms and steps.

All returned attention drops with section/exact quote, a one-sentence takeaway, three remembered points, unmet needs, terms that interrupted reading and the next action. They did not assess the science or make a publication recommendation.
