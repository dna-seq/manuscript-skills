# Real-manuscript storytelling smoke review

This run reviewed the actual current v0.5 manuscript, not an evaluation fixture. Three fresh agents read only the manuscript export, their seat instructions and the figure files. Their full actual responses are retained in readers/. These are simulated perspectives, not human-participant evidence or scientific referee reports.

## What the readers understood

All three understood local genome annotation, source-linked findings and ancestry-referenced polygenic scores. All three specifically remembered the worked FOXO3 example and the fact that a weight of 0.475 does not predict extra years of life. The concrete example provided a foothold in a component-heavy paper. This does not establish that the revision is preferred over an earlier version; the earlier panel was separate and this is not a controlled experiment.

## Where they still lost attention

All three described losing the practical task amid implementation names, artifact terminology and PRS matching/ancestry mechanics. The skimmer and both full readers also found Figure 3's obsolete-display disclaimer disruptive. The potential user could not confidently establish first-run downloads, disk requirements or whether their file's genome build was supported. The outsider wanted a complete question-to-result-to-next-action journey and a worked interpretation of model disagreement.

## Disagreements and limits

The skimmer did not inspect figure assets and could not find a demonstration starting point during the permitted skim. The potential user and outsider viewed all three figures and retained more of the report contents, yet still lacked a complete practical journey. These differences are retained rather than averaged into a readability score.

The generated reading export includes an internal opening draft notice. The potential user explicitly reacted to it before the Abstract. That is a real draft artifact, but it limits inference about how the final submitted paper will read. An external-review reading copy should omit internal author planning notes. Raw input snapshots and this actual reading export are retained separately.

## Measures

The command log compares the current real manuscript to the preserved pre-edit version from this session: 5,225 to 5,482 body words, average sentence length 24.4 to 23.7, and the share over 35 words 16.9% to 14.8%. Caveat-ending sentences increased slightly (14.1% to 14.3%). That is mixed evidence, not a blanket readability improvement.

## Coverage

This is a bounded reader-panel and metrics smoke review. It did not repeat the full comparison with neighboring papers and exemplars or the full story-science/journal/prose adjudication. The manuscript masters were not edited during this smoke run. Status is partial for the full manuscript-story workflow; that label must remain visible even though the three-reader evidence and artifact handover succeeded.
