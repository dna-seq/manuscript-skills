The [moderation policy](https://info.arxiv.org/help/moderation/index.html) was read live in Chrome on 19 October 2026.

This order is a fallback after a decline, not a plan to post the same manuscript twice. bioRxiv says a manuscript already present on another preprint server will not be posted. ## What can be prepared for all active choices

**Adapt now.** Explain the authoring contribution independently of Paper 1; preserve real output, provenance, limitations and a reproducible evaluation; give reviewers a working installation, test data and evidence behind each result. Provide software/data availability, author contributions, funding and competing-interest information. State that the plugin authors knowledge and does not process individual genomes. Declare and cite overlapping companion work. These shared items can be prepared without changing the paper's scientific direction.

**Choose per venue.** BMC asks for a structured abstract of at most 350 words and a Software-article structure; Bioinformatics recommends a 150-word structured abstract and an Original Paper of about 5,000 words or seven journal pages. Do not shorten the whole manuscript to that limit before choosing. The generated common-requirements file initially promoted the only known body limit into “Adapt now”; that is a helper limitation, not this report's recommendation.

**At the end, once code, data and results are final.** Archive the exact software and evaluation corpus, add persistent identifiers and complete the factual availability/declaration text. Replace the EASRP anonymity fields and anonymous repository URLs with the final author/repository information. Record exact versions for each paper; do not make Paper 1's archived benchmark version depend on the latest Paper 2 plugin.

The strictest writing rule is Bioinformatics' restriction on drafting from a “prompt text,” with disclosure in the cover letter, manuscript and supplement. Its policy permits AI as the research technique, which suits Paper 2's subject. That does not automatically permit AI to draft the article itself. The actual Paper 2 writing-assistance account was not established from author testimony in this run; repository tool mentions do not establish it. Any conflict with actual use is the authors' venue decision. The live [BMC risk framework](https://link.springer.com/brands/bmc/editorial-policies) permits support under human oversight and disclosure and does not reproduce the catalogue's older blanket Results/Discussion restriction.
