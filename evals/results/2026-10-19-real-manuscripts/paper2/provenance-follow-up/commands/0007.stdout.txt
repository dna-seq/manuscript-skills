    Three runs scored, and the paragraph says what they are worth
    
    Runs 2 and 3 landed. The table is full: all three recovered the variant the
    paper supports, cited both it and the study it replicates, agreed on direction,
    and introduced no decoy. Two matched the reference on every genotype row
    including the homozygous row that asserts nothing because neither cohort saw a
    carrier, and all three independently declined to propagate the seed paper's own
    misdescription of the variant as stop-gained.
    
    The paragraph does not boast, and the reason is a deflation one of the runs
    volunteered about itself. The validator names the missing genotype explicitly
    and a written rule says a zero weight is a claim while a blank is not — so
    agreement partly measures the guidance rather than the runs. Convergence shows
    the workflow is prescriptive enough to produce consistent output from
    independent runs, which is weaker than, and must not be read as, independent
    confirmation that the output is right. That sentence is in the paper.
    
    The residual variance is one cell: suggestive versus not_significant on a p of
    0.07, two runs against one, from the same number. A vocabulary ambiguity already
    filed upstream, and an aggregate score would have hidden it.
    
    Both runs were integrity-checked against their own transcripts, not their
    self-reports: no access to the reference, the sibling runs, or any registry read
    that could return a published module. One run did list data/interim/ and saw five
    directory names, no contents; it disclosed this unprompted before I asked.
