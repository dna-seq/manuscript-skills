# A blind manuscript-improve run on a reviewed draft, compared with its human review

Date: 2026-10-22. Skills: this repository at the 0.8.0 release candidate. Model: Claude Opus 5.5 for the
coordinator and every stage agent. Grader of the comparison: a separate Claude Opus 5.5 agent, after the
run's inventory was frozen.

## What was run

The full manuscript-improve workflow on the v0.3 draft of Paper 1 (Just-DNA-Lite), the version a co-author
reviewed in September 2026. The run was blind: its agents received the v0.3 text, a snapshot of the
project's code at the draft's commit (without `docs/manuscript` or git history) and the companion paper of
that time, and nothing written later, including the review. The authors' priorities for journal selection
(fast publication, impact factor above 3) were given, as an author would give them. The full run,
dashboard and comparison stay with the manuscript project
(`just-dna-lite/docs/manuscript/v0.3/reviews/2026-10-22-blind-improve/`), not in this public repository:
they quote the unpublished draft and the reviewer's comments.

Stages: integrity, references, reporting and availability, policy fit, journal selection, three
simulated referees for the three chosen journals with a defence pass and adjudication, a reader panel
(three default seats, then six readers the authors chose, reading in their own order), prose edit under the
conservative policy, journal adaptation as proposals, verification and the dashboard. The final manifest
check passed; policy fit and the story stage are recorded as partial, with their reasons.

## Results

- 97 distinct problems confirmed (37 major) and 12 candidates, from 632 raw signals; 38 found by two or more
  stages and counted once; 53 flags dismissed with a reason.
- 7 routine edits applied; 88 proposals, 12 passages with competing proposals from different stages.
- Referees: major revision at Genes and Frontiers in Bioinformatics from all three; at BioData Mining,
  reject from two and major revision from one. The defence answered part of 24 concerns and none in full;
  adjudication confirmed 33, downgraded 7, refuted none.

Compared with 125 problems people raised in v0.3 (the co-author's comments and tracked edits, and the
authors' later fixes):

| Origin | Items | Matched | Partly | Not caught |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Reviewer's comments | 38 | 8 | 12 | 18 |
| Reviewer's tracked edits (mostly wording) | 26 | 5 | 2 | 19 |
| Authors' own findings | 28 | 20 | 3 | 5 |
| Benchmark rerun | 13 | 10 | 3 | 0 |
| Journal fit | 20 | 9 | 4 | 7 |
| **All** | **125** | **52** | **24** | **49** |

Of the 80 substantive items, 65 were caught at least in part. Twelve items came from the authors' later
AI-assisted revision pass and all twelve matched; without them, 40 of 113 matched in full. The run found
39 confirmed problems the human review did not raise.

## What it changed in the skills (0.8.0)

- Misses: the integrity check now compares the paper's description of shipped content with the project's
  data files, not only the code, and asks whether a benchmark used the input file the paper cites (with a
  way to read a deposited VCF's header without downloading it); the reporting check asks for the provenance
  of curated content and whether a comparator got the same inputs.
- Scripts: `scan.py` no longer accepts a title match from another journal, volume and pages as "fits"
  (the run's invented reference); `runs.py exec` runs where it is called; `venues.py ladder` warns when
  first-decision figures count different papers.
- Rules: delegated stages that cannot write files hand the report to the coordinator; referee seats may run
  in the background when the host reports completion; proposal stages reconcile with confirmed findings;
  parallel stages browse in their own tabs; reference comparisons run blind with sources pinned to the
  draft's date.
- Readers: seats are configurable per project; reading is a funnel along each reader's own path, and a
  reader who left cannot try or cite the work.
- Visualization: the infographic became a dashboard with the annotated manuscript, the journals, competing
  proposals, the reading map and the comparison with a reference review, collected from each stage's
  `findings.json`.

Each fix has a regression test where a script changed. Full offline suite: 1,228 tests passed.

## Limits

One paper, one run, one grader. The reference is one review plus the authors' fixes, not ground truth; a
problem the run found and the review did not is not judged. The coordinator was Claude, so the merges and
the coverage statuses are its judgement, recorded in the run's merge file. Two stages read web pages
updated after the draft; their findings do not count as matches.
